About us

Built for the farmer's phone

HarvestHub helps Ghana's smallholder farmers plant on time, store without losses and sell at a fair price.

🌾 Farmers

Today's prices, planting windows and storage on a basic phone and slow data.

🚚 Traders & aggregators

Prices across regions to plan buying and a directory to find sellers.

🧑‍🏫 Extension officers

Reliable information to share with the farmers they support.

Our story (sample company history)

2023 · Founded in Accra after a survey of 600 farmers in Techiman showed most never knew the day's price.
2024 · Launched the SMS price service in Bono East and the Northern Region.
2025 · Added cold-room and warehouse listings and the planting calendar across 10 regions.
2026 · Reached 48,000 registered farmers and moved to a fast, secure cloud platform on AWS built with ThetaCloud.
