Frequently asked questions

Is HarvestHub really free?

Yes. Every service on this page is free. SMS alerts and the toll-free helpline cost you nothing.

How do I get prices by SMS?

Send PRICE followed by your crop, for example PRICE MAIZE, to 0000. You can also dial *XXX*00# and choose Prices.

How do rain alerts work?

Send RAIN followed by your district, for example RAIN TECHIMAN, to 0000. We text you when planting rains start, stall or turn heavy.

Where do the prices come from?

Our market agents record prices in 6 markets every morning before 6:00 AM, and we publish the averages and each market's price.

Which languages do you support?

The helpline answers in English, Twi, Hausa and Ewe. SMS replies are available in English and Twi.
